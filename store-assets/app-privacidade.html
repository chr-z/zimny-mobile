Privacy Policy — ZIMNY App

Effective date: August 24, 2026

This policy explains how the ZIMNY Magazine mobile application ("the app") handles your information. In short: the app is designed so that we do not collect your personal data.

1. What the app does NOT collect

  • We do not require an account to use the app;
  • We do not collect, transmit or store your name, email, phone number or location on our servers;
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs;
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

2. Information stored on YOUR device only

The following preferences are saved locally on your phone (using standard device storage) and never leave it:

  • Saved/bookmarked articles;
  • Language preference (English, Portuguese, Spanish);
  • Theme and font size preferences.

Deleting the app removes this data permanently.

3. Content loaded from our servers

The app displays news articles, events, photos, podcast episodes and videos published by ZIMNY Media Corporation. Loading this content makes standard network requests to our infrastructure (zimnymagazine.com) and trusted media providers (such as YouTube embeds). These requests may include your IP address as is inherent to any internet connection; we do not store or profile them.

4. Third-party links

The app may open external links (for example, our WhatsApp support or social media). Once you leave the app, those services' own privacy policies apply.

5. Children

The app contains general-audience editorial content and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from any user.

6. Changes

If future versions of the app introduce data collection (for example, optional accounts), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and you will be informed in the release notes.
